1. The Lithium Map (EV Transition)

Domestic battery mineral reserves are being fast-tracked through private block auctions:

  • Chhattisgarh (Katghora Block): Premier Korba battery mineral block auctioned as composite Lithium & REE.
  • Jammu & Kashmir (Salal-Haimna): Reasi district deposit with 5.9 Million tonnes inferred lithium resources.
  • Karnataka (Mandya District): Hard-rock spodumene reserve explored by the Atomic Minerals Directorate.

2. REE Coastal Corridors & Magnet Scheme

India holds the world's 3rd largest rare earth reserves (13.15M tonnes monazite sands). The Budget 2026–27 launched dedicated corridors co-locating mining with ₹7,280 Cr REPM Magnet Manufacturing (6,000 MTPA capacity).

  • East Coast Corridor (Odisha & AP): Monazite sands processed by IREL OSCOM.
  • South Coast Corridor (Kerala & TN): Neodymium & Praseodymium rich sands for EV motors.
  • Hard-Rock Ores: Carbonatite complexes mapped in Gujarat and Rajasthan.
